Atmospheric introduces a variety of exotic biomes across many climates of the world!

#### Rainforest

Found in similar areas as Jungles, Rainforests bring a whole new tropical vibe, with Rosewood and Morado trees creating a lively ecosystem. Here you'll also find Passion Fruit, which can be eaten to spit seeds at your foes, as well as Tetras, an ambient fish with a few rare variants!

#### Scrubland

Scrublands are a similar ecosystem to the Dunes, but with more shrubbery and a different set of sand. Scrublands are the only biomes where Cochineal spawn, a strange red bug that hops around the biome!
